Method: `fitPlane` sorts the selected points lexicographically by (x, y, z) (`canonicalOrder`) before RANSAC and the
least-squares refinement. Equal point sets give the identical array, so sampling and the floating-point summation order
are identical; points with equal coordinates are interchangeable. O(n log n), one copy of the selection.
