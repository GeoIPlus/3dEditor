## What the measured path does

`MOVE_EDGE` in plane terms (spec §10.4): target line -> hard "point on plane" conditions of the movable incident faces;
fixed incident faces checked; `FIXED_POINT` vertices on movable faces are hard points; per-plane solve with the
objective of [3D-ADR-0005](../decisions/adr/3D-ADR-0005-solver-approach.md) (O1); every vertex of a changed face
re-derived from its defining planes (no vertex written); FAST_PREVIEW of the affected faces (vertex-plane residual,
edge length, face area, ring self-crossing, face-face crossing with every face sharing no vertex); `command-result`
with patch, lineage and report; Ajv validation of request and reply.

## What it does not do (the margin has to cover this)

- Stage 2 of the solver (coupled / nonlinear constraints, soft constraints, minimal conflict sets): 3D-13.
- Full COMMIT_VALIDATION (stitching, orientation, regression against the previous version, solid checks): 3D-12.
- Face-face tests between faces that share a vertex; coplanar overlaps.
- Storage I/O, network, authorisation, idempotency store, concurrency (one request at a time).
- COMMIT and import latency (v0.3 §11 rows 2 and 4): not measured.
